Join us for a weekend of fall foraying in the temperate rainforest of
Tryon Creek. Expect chanterelles, boletes, and a wide range of fall
fruiting species. Saturday is a guided foray followed by a communal
identification table; Sunday is a beginner-friendly walk and workshop
on safe identification practices.

Bring a basket, a knife, and rain gear — this is the Pacific Northwest,
after all.
