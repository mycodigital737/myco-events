A four-day, biennial gathering on a 40-acre farm — part arts festival, part
outdoor science lab. More than 25 researchers, artists, growers, chefs,
filmmakers and educators lead upwards of 45 beginner-friendly workshops and
demonstrations spanning cultivation, identification, fermentation, natural
dyes, illustration, microscopy and fungal ecology, alongside live
entertainment, citizen science and nighttime nature experiences.

Day passes start at $25; kids 12 and under attend free, with free
childcare available during workshop sessions.
